## What it is

slayply is a tool you run on your own machine to take the grind out of job applications. You bring your own API keys, give it your résumés and your preferences, and it builds a personality from your facts. Then, on any job description, one click does three things: it reads the JD, checks how well you fit, and applies, answering every question the recruiter asks in a human voice.

## How it works

- **You bring the keys.** Your API keys stay with you, on your machine.
- **A fact-based personality.** It's generated from what's true about you, from your résumés and what you tell it, so the answers don't invent things you never did.
- **Résumés and preferences.** Upload several résumés and say what you're looking for.
- **One click.** Read the JD, check the fit, apply, and answer the recruiter's questions.

## The honest bit

I built it in about an hour of monitored vibe-coding, to solve my own lazy need. I watched what the AI wrote and steered it, rather than letting it run unattended. I've tested it with Gemini API keys in production and it works like a charm. The UX still needs work, and that's the next thing I'll improve.
